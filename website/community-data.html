DATA ARCHITECTURE

Public insight without publishing protected records.

PUBLIC

Community & Village

Qualified aggregate planning baselines, geographic reach and public community information.

PUBLIC / OPT-IN

Professional Network

Public professional and mentor participation only where appropriate and voluntarily shared.

PUBLIC

Education Pathways

Learning pathways, public opportunities and aggregate development indicators.

PUBLIC

Research & Knowledge

Verified research, public resources and institutional learning.

CONTROLLED

Operational Services

Only services explicitly approved for public exposure are published.

PROTECTED

Person & Household Records

Personal, household, safeguarding and case-management data remain outside the public layer.

DATA → ACTION → IMPACT

Community data should help people, not merely fill a database.

The public layer is designed to support planning, learning, participation and accountability while the protected operational layer preserves privacy and safeguarding boundaries.
