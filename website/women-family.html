PUBLIC / PROTECTED BOUNDARY

Useful public access without exposing sensitive family data.

PUBLIC

Approved opportunities, learning resources, general wellbeing information, participation routes and verified services can be published.

PROTECTED

Household profiles, child records, safeguarding cases, health information, financial-need assessments and individual support records remain outside the public layer.

VERIFICATION GATE

A support form or service is not exposed merely because it exists. Public classification and website exposure must be verified first.

SAFE PARTICIPATION

Public calls to action route people only to currently available public gateways; no private case intake is simulated on this page.
