PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE STANDARD

Useful in public. Protected when sensitive.

The public layer may contain reviewed research, approved publications, public learning resources and appropriate aggregate evidence. Person-level records, confidential assessments, restricted internal documents, safeguarding information and unpublished sensitive research remain outside the public dataset.
