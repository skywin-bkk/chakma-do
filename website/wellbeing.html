PUBLIC / PROTECTED BOUNDARY

Help people find support without publishing sensitive records

Public layer

Approved guidance, verified service descriptions, public resources, aggregate indicators and explicitly website-exposed intake routes.

Protected layer

Individual health information, household vulnerability, child or safeguarding cases, beneficiary records, assessments, complaints and confidential referrals remain private.

Verification rule

A service or form is not published merely because it exists. It must be explicitly classified for public website exposure before a direct intake link is surfaced.
